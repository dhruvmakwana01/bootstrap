Explore Destinations

Discover beautiful destinations across India and explore the most popular places to visit in each location.

Manali

Manali is a beautiful mountain destination known for snow-covered landscapes, valleys, adventure activities and peaceful surroundings. It is a popular destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

Places to Visit in Manali

Solang Valley

Famous for snow activities, skiing, paragliding and scenic mountain views.

Rohtang Pass

A spectacular mountain pass surrounded by snow-covered Himalayan landscapes.

Hadimba Temple

An ancient wooden temple surrounded by beautiful cedar forests.

Old Manali

A charming area filled with cafes, local shops and peaceful mountain views.

Goa

Goa is known for its beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese architecture and relaxed coastal atmosphere. It is a popular destination for beach lovers and travellers.

Places to Visit in Goa

Baga Beach

A lively beach popular for water sports, restaurants and nightlife.

Fort Aguada

A historic Portuguese fort offering beautiful views of the Arabian Sea.

Dudhsagar Falls

A spectacular waterfall surrounded by lush green forests and hills.

Palolem Beach

A scenic beach known for its calm waters, palm trees and relaxing atmosphere.

Jaipur

Jaipur, the Pink City of India, is famous for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, colorful markets and rich Rajasthani culture.

Places to Visit in Jaipur

Amber Fort

A magnificent historic fort known for its architecture and hilltop views.

Hawa Mahal

Jaipur's iconic palace famous for its unique windows and pink architecture.

City Palace

A beautiful royal complex showcasing Jaipur's history and traditional design.

Jantar Mantar

A historic astronomical observatory featuring large scientific instruments.

Agra

Agra is a historic city famous for the Taj Mahal, Mughal architecture and fascinating monuments. It is one of India's most visited heritage destinations.

Places to Visit in Agra

Taj Mahal

One of India's most famous monuments, known for its stunning white marble design.

Agra Fort

A historic Mughal fort featuring impressive palaces and architecture.

Mehtab Bagh

A peaceful garden offering beautiful views of the Taj Mahal across the river.

Itmad-ud-Daulah

An elegant Mughal-era tomb known for its detailed marble decoration.

Kerala

Kerala is known for its peaceful backwaters, lush greenery, beaches and beautiful hill stations. It offers a relaxing experience surrounded by nature.

Places to Visit in Kerala

Alleppey

Famous for peaceful backwaters, houseboats and beautiful waterways.

Munnar

A scenic hill station surrounded by tea plantations and green mountains.

Kovalam Beach

A beautiful coastal destination known for its beaches and relaxing atmosphere.

Wayanad

A green and peaceful destination filled with forests, waterfalls and hills.
